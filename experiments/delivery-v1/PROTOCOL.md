# Delivery bench v1 — protocol fixed before simulation

Question: which acoustic delivery contrasts can the existing RMS input bridge and frozen fly.ai model distinguish beyond direct injection?

Sources: the two previously published PCM WAVs of Blake's The Fly. These are the source waveforms for this experiment, already processed by the earlier edition. Their original provenance and earlier normalization are retained. No new TTS or speech analysis. Human MP3 source remains in examples/blake-sayers/original.mp3. Fixed whole-waveform linear scaling to a common feasible RMS, with a 0.95 peak ceiling; no compression or clipping. PCM16 rounding tolerance is measured. Real performance durations are not equalized.

Conditions: synthetic reference; human performance; independently rerun exact reference; polarity-inverted reference (different PCM, identical RMS injections); reference with existing inter-line digital silence redistributed alternately into short/long pauses (total duration and speech samples preserved); alternating line amplitude multipliers 0.5/1.5, then common RMS; reversed order of complete synthetic line-plus-gap segments (contents and duration preserved). The latter three are diagnostic manipulations, not natural performances. Pauses use generated digital gaps, not inferred human word boundaries. Text/line identities are display metadata only.

Simulator: existing SimulationRunner / upstream FlyBrain; 166,700-neuron MaleCNS, unchanged weights, parameters, timestep, warmup, baseline and one-second tail. All conditions use the same seed set. Equal-duration zero-input runs are shared only within a seed and exact frame length. Repeated/polarity controls are actually simulated independently; never copied from reference. Raw spikes, population counts, provenance and input hashes saved locally.

Pilot: seeds 64,65 on the two performances and their silence controls. Choose total replication from measured runtime and pilot spread, not a significance stopping rule: 8 seeds (64–71) if projected complete bench is under 45 minutes; otherwise 6 (64–69). Both are exploratory small ensembles, not powered biological studies. Keep pilot seeds in the final descriptive ensemble; disclose this. No early stopping for a favorable effect.

Prespecified primary populations: direct JON postsynaptic partners excluding injected JONs; pooled descending neurons. Secondary: full network; injected JONs as manipulation check only. Metrics: audio-window mean stimulus-minus-silence rate; RMS magnitude of the 100 ms binned difference trajectory; one-second mean tail difference. For rendition contrasts, subtract these quantities within each seed. Report individual paired differences, mean, sample SD, positive/negative/zero counts and paired absolute-mean/SD ratio (descriptive, not significance). Report 100 ms elapsed-time ensemble mean and min–max bands. No temporal stretching or extrapolation between different durations; line navigation seeks corresponding source lines at their actual times.

Exploratory population recruitment: annotated non-input cell types with at least 10 neurons; largest absolute mean paired rate differences, including across-seed direction agreement. This is an exploratory ranking, not a significance test or a behavioral label; populations may overlap monitored groups. Positive/negative means rate change, not affective valence.

Decision: exact controls must match raw spike arrays and counts. A candidate delivery effect requires a consistent downstream paired difference beyond its across-seed spread; mixed-sign and null results must remain visible. Even a consistent difference is a model effect under this amplitude transducer, not a biological norm. Matching waveform RMS does not match mean frame RMS, capped injection, total duration or integrated dose; report those explicitly. Diagnostic equal-duration contrasts help separate temporal structure from whole-waveform energy. No LLM or poem-aware literary inference in analysis.
