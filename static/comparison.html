This short account is generated by transparent rules from paired response statistics alone. It receives no poem text. “More activity” does not establish enjoyment, fear, or a performed action.

Method and downloadable evidence

These are new level-matched experiments. The listening chamber retains its earlier single-seed recordings. The two should not be confused.

The original inputs here are the previously published PCM files; their prior normalization and source provenance are retained. Diagnostic variants alter synthetic line segments or verified digital silence. All simulations retain the original connectome and model parameters.

Full spike arrays are retained in the local experiment archive. The public count archive contains per-step global/group counts and phase counts for every annotated cell type, including silence controls. The manifest records local raw-file hashes.
