METHOD / What are we actually doing?

A fly listens. We interpret.

The poem was rendered as audio. No semantic representation was provided to the fly. A published model of antennal mechanics filters the sound; its displacement envelope drives the Johnston’s-organ neurons used by fly.ai.

The connection from modeled antennal motion to neural input is an explicit engineering assumption. This is a literature-informed auditory model, not a fully validated fly ear. The MaleCNS connectome weights remain frozen.

RESPONSE comes from simulated neural activity. READING interprets those measurements without receiving the poem text. This computational artwork is not evidence that a fly understands poetry or experiences a particular emotion.

What you see

The readers’ movements are theatrical animation. The fly body is a posed anatomical model, not a simulated behavioral response. Amber points show a fixed sample of real simulated spikes from one recorded run; the activity trace averages four runs. Both performances use the same scales.

Recordings are level-matched without compression. Their durations, pauses and internal dynamics still differ. Human stanza boundaries are approximate. Noise seeds represent model variability, not different biological flies.

The body is from NeuroMechFly / FlyGym. Robot: Kokoro af_sarah. Human: Denny Sayers, LibriVox, 2006. Reader figures are fictional stage characters.
