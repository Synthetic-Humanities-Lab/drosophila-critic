RESPONSE / measured differences

Population Temporal difference Background variability Agreement between halves Declared criterion

Temporal difference: RMS of the average paired trace. Variability: RMS of its across-seed standard deviation, both Hz per neuron. Agreement: cosine similarity between the first four and last four repetitions. The criterion requires difference > variability and agreement > 0.5. This rule was fixed before these runs; it is a descriptive check, not a statistical significance test.

FUNCTIONAL IMPLICATION / what does this mean for the modeled fly?

Direct JON partners are cells connected immediately downstream of the injected auditory neurons. A difference here means the disturbance has propagated beyond the cells we directly stimulate. Descending neurons provide a separate, broad measure of activity associated with output toward the body. A rate difference alone does not specify a movement or feeling.

READING / an affective interpretation

The interpreter receives only response measurements and temporal positions. It has no poem text or performer identity. Sound, sensory mapping, neural model, measurement and prose each make a different part of the encounter available.

ONE PAUSE / the next resumption

We moved 180 ms of verified silence from after source line 8 to after line 4. Every speech sample, total silence and duration remain unchanged. Source line 5 is blank; line 6 is the first displaced spoken onset.

For each rendition, compare 300 ms after its own line-6 onset with 300 ms before, subtract matched silence, then subtract the reference contrast. The injection values in these aligned windows are identical; earlier histories and absolute times differ. This is an onset-associated response measure, not a measured causal latency.

Method, translations and downloadable evidence

The same frozen MaleCNS / fly.ai model receives the existing amplitude-based JON input. No antennal mechanics or frequency sensitivity was added. The change is experimental: new paired runs, controlled temporal interventions, declared measurements, and a longer observation after sound.

Distinguishing rearranged input does not by itself demonstrate memory or complex temporal processing: a response that follows the changing drive can also pass this check. The localized pause probes a narrower contrast. We have not calibrated these rates to a living fly’s actions.
