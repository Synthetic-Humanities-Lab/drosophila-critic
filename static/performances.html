        METHOD · What this apparatus can register
        
      
          Audio is reduced to amplitude over 20 ms frames and supplied to the
          138 Johnston’s-organ neurons used by fly.ai. The original MaleCNS
          connectome remains frozen. No words or semantic representation enter
          the neural simulation. This receiver can register pauses and amplitude
          structure; it does not reproduce the full mechanics or frequency
          selectivity of a fly’s ear.
        
      
        
      
          Each performance has an equal-duration silence control with the same
          initialization and simulator seed. Four seeds measure variability in
          this model, not differences among biological flies. Equal RMS controls
          one acoustic property; longer recordings still produce longer
          exposure. The response readouts are measurements. The reading is a
          conditional interpretation of those measurements, not evidence that a
          fly understands poetry.
