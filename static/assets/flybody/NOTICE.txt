Modified for The Drosophila Critic: original body/joint hierarchy exported to GLB;
coincident mesh vertices welded, smooth normals regenerated, display materials
adapted. Physical dimensions are retained in centimetres; camera framing changes
apparent size. Motion is a separate MuJoCo simulation, not baked animation.

This female anatomical exemplar and the MaleCNS male nervous system are separate
models. There is no claim of sex-specific anatomical registration.

Frozen policies and published wingbeat data have a separate GPL 3.0+ license;
see ../body-v1/NOTICE.txt. MuJoCo uses Apache 2.0; see ../../vendor/mujoco/LICENSE.txt.
