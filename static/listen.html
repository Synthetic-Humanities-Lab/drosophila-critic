METHOD / A browser simulation

Original MaleCNS weights, original fly.ai update, 20 ms timestep, PCG64 noise and seed 1101. Four controlled runs: synthetic reference, your recording and their matched silence controls. This is one paired repetition, not the four-seed published experiment. Human recordings are not transcribed or automatically aligned by line.

The published antennal displacement filter feeds the same 138 JO-A/B targets. Its neural coupling is an engineering assumption. Audio is decoded at 48 kHz, linearly level matched without compression, and then converted into acoustic input. Browser decoding may differ slightly from the Python resampler.

No feelings, preference, understanding or physical movement are inferred. Browser execution was qualified on the development desktop only. Reduced performance, memory limits, cache eviction and background throttling remain possible on other devices.
